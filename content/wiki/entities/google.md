# Google

**Type:** Organisation  
**Also known as:** Alphabet (parent); Google LLC

## Overview

Google is the world's dominant search engine and one of the leading frontier AI labs (through DeepMind and Google Brain, now merged into Google DeepMind). It invented the Transformer architecture (2017, "Attention Is All You Need"), pioneered TPU custom silicon, built Waymo (autonomous vehicles), and now competes directly in the LLM race with its Gemini model family. Its vertical integration — owning TPUs, data centers, the model, and the distribution surface (Search, Android, Chrome, YouTube) — is a key competitive differentiator.

## The Transformer Paradox

The Transformer was invented inside Google Brain, was not Google's main priority, and ultimately powered the companies (OpenAI, Anthropic) that now compete directly with Google. Sundar frames this as an inherent feature of consumer internet — surprises are inevitable. His response is to move fast in the present, not to mourn the past.

## Speed Philosophy

Google's latency culture is extreme: sub-teams have millisecond-level budgets. Shipping something that shaves 3ms earns credits against your budget. This is reflected in the Gemini Flash/Pro split — Flash delivers ~90% of Pro capability at dramatically lower latency and cost.
