# Flock

**Type:** Organisation (product/company)
**Also known as:** Flock Safety

## Overview

Flock is an AI-powered public-safety surveillance company in the a16z portfolio. Its system ingests municipal and traffic camera feeds and uses AI to identify vehicles in real time — both by license plate and, distinctively, by recognising a car from its physical markings even without a plate. Sold to city governments and deployed across the US; credited with solving and preventing crimes daily.

## Appearances in Sources

- [[wiki/sources/marc-andreessen-jre-2501]] — Austin disabled Flock on privacy/surveillance grounds and could not catch a teen shooting-spree until suspects crossed into an adjacent Flock-enabled town, which tagged them on arrival. Andreessen uses it as the central case for AI surveillance trade-offs (crime-solving vs. mass-surveillance/abuse risk; "woke" disparate-impact objection vs. victims also from disadvantaged groups).
