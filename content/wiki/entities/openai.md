# OpenAI

**Type:** Organisation  
**Also known as:** OpenAI LP (capped profit); transitioning to for-profit

## Overview

OpenAI is the maker of ChatGPT, DALL-E, Codex, GPT-4, and the GPT/o series models. Founded in 2015 as a nonprofit with a stated mission of ensuring AGI benefits all of humanity, it has evolved into a capped-profit company (recently transitioning to a more standard for-profit structure). It is one of the two dominant frontier AI labs alongside [[wiki/entities/anthropic]], and significantly ahead on compute scale.

## Competitive Position (from Dylan Patel)

OpenAI has more compute than Anthropic. This is the key variable in Dylan's analysis: Anthropic leads on current model quality (Mythos), but OpenAI's compute advantage means it will reach equivalent model tiers sooner and be able to serve more demand. When it does, it doesn't need the best model — it just needs to be "good enough" with enough capacity to serve everyone Anthropic cannot.

## The ChatGPT Moment

Per Sam Altman: ChatGPT was not OpenAI's most technically impressive product, but it was the first thing people could *feel*. That moment — when people could develop their own intuition about AI — was the true mass adoption trigger. Prior achievements (robot hands, game-playing AI, blog posts) produced no real-world adoption.
