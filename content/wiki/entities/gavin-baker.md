# Gavin Baker

**Type:** Person  
**Also known as:** Technology investor; known for encyclopedic knowledge of AI infrastructure economics

## Overview

Gavin Baker is a technology investor who appears regularly on Invest Like The Best to provide investor-grade analysis of AI infrastructure, chip economics, and the competitive dynamics between GPU makers (Nvidia), ASIC producers (Google TPU, Amazon Trainium), and the four leading frontier AI labs. He is notable for being one of the few public commentators who combines deep semiconductor knowledge with capital allocation analysis and a practitioner-level understanding of how these dynamics feed back into market outcomes.

## Intellectual Contributions

- **Reasoning models saved AI**: Had reasoning not emerged in late 2024, there would have been zero AI progress from mid-2024 through Gemini 3 due to the Blackwell deployment gap.
- **Two new post-training scaling laws**: RLVR (reinforcement learning with verified rewards) and test-time compute. Both multiplicative with pre-training scaling.
- **Lowest-cost token producer matters**: AI is the first technology where being the cheapest matters strategically; Google used this to "suck the economic oxygen out" of the AI ecosystem.
- **Verification = automation**: Anything verifiable can be automated via RL — the key filter for identifying which functions AI will take over.
- **Fortune 500 adoption curve**: Same lag as cloud (startups first; Fortune 500 ~5 years later). CH Robinson as first quantitative non-tech example.
- **Four-lab gap widening**: Reasoning flywheel + internal checkpoints are making it harder (not easier) for Meta, Microsoft, Amazon to catch up.
