# Gokul Rajaram

**Type:** Person  
**Also known as:** Product executive and angel investor; built revenue engines at Google (AdSense), Facebook/Meta, Square, DoorDash

## Overview

Gokul Rajaram is one of the most experienced product leaders in Silicon Valley, having built the advertising and product systems that generated billions in revenue at Google, Facebook, Square, and DoorDash. He is a prolific angel investor in ~700 companies and a leading thinker on how AI is restructuring the discipline of product management. His core thesis: deterministic software is dying, non-deterministic agentic software is taking over, and the only future-proof human skill is **judgment**.

## Intellectual Contributions

- **Non-deterministic software**: AI agents produce variable outputs for identical inputs — this fundamentally changes who owns quality control (PMs must own evals, not just specs).
- **Five durability pillars**: Network effects; financial control points; hardware; unique scarce asset; system-of-record ownership.
- **Seat-pricing vulnerability**: Zendesk-style seat-based pricing is uniquely exposed to AI displacement; outcome-based pricing is the survival model.
- **Migration moat**: To displace a system of record, an AI startup must first invest 1-2 years building a seamless migration tool.
- **PM:engineer ratio expanding**: From 1:3 historically toward 1:20 and beyond as AI automates implementation.
