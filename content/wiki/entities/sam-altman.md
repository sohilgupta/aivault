# Sam Altman

**Type:** Person  
**Also known as:** CEO of OpenAI

## Overview

Sam Altman is the CEO of [[wiki/entities/openai]], having joined as President in 2019 and become CEO. He was famously fired by the board in November 2023 and reinstated within days. He is the primary public voice of OpenAI — more product and narrative-focused than technically deep, with a strong emphasis on making AI accessible and on the mission of ensuring AI benefits everyone.

## Worldview (from sources)

- **"Feel it to believe it"**: ChatGPT succeeded not because it was technically most impressive but because people could *feel* it. Democratising the feeling is the key to social adoption.
- **Personal AGI that knows you**: The next frontier — AI that has your full context so you never re-explain yourself. "Tremendously excited" about this.
- **Compute strategy**: OpenAI pursued aggressive compute acquisition (Oracle, CoreWeave, SoftBank, Microsoft, Amazon/Tranium) — now validated. Anthropic wishes it had done the same.
- **The LLM path is optimistic**: Same conclusion as Dario — the shift away from competitive-agent AGI to LLM-rooted AI is a fundamentally more optimistic and human-values-connected path.

## On Elon Musk

The breaking point with Elon was his demand for *absolute control* of OpenAI — not equity, not partnership, but complete authority. Sam, Greg, and Ilya said no. OpenAI has been silent for years; the lawsuit forces the real story to be told, which Sam welcomes.
