# Jonathan Ross

**Type:** Person
**Also known as:** inventor of the Google TPU; founder/CEO of Groq; now an NVIDIA executive

## Overview

Jonathan Ross built the first Google Tensor Processing Unit (TPU) — the chip that powered DeepMind's AlphaGo victory over Lee Sedol — then left Google to found [[wiki/entities/groq]] on a single contrarian bet: **fast inference makes models smarter and would become economically central.** After a ~$20B NVIDIA/Groq deal (2026) he joined [[wiki/entities/nvidia]] as a senior executive. He is a delegation-first, question-driven leader who candidly calls his early self "one of the world's worst leaders."

## Intellectual Contributions (from sources)

- **Fast inference → smarter models.** More compute/latency per decision enables deeper search and creative moves (AlphaGo move 37) unreachable on slower hardware.
- **GPU+LPU complementarity.** Attention is compute-bound (GPU); weight application is memory-throughput-bound (LPU). The hard part is generation ("writing"), not prefill ("reading").
- **Intentional leadership** — "I intend to..." (from Marquet's *Turn the Ship Around*) replaces inviting pessimism with announcing direction; keeps autonomous teams moving.
- **Under-constrained goals** — one brutally-clear objective ("25M tokens/sec" on a challenge coin) lets creative people surprise you.
- **Hire for negatives (select), grow for positives.** Distinct mental modes; conflating them causes bad hires.
- **Groq bonds** — salary-for-equity swaps that saved Groq from insolvency; "put everyone's hands on the steering wheel."
- **Manufactured discontent** — the engine of sustained effort; currently discontent with "the lack of compute in the world," framed morally (delayed cures for cancer/aging).
- **Return on luck** (Jim Collins) — the best don't get more luck, they seize it better.
