# Anthropic

**Type:** Organisation  
**Also known as:** Anthropic PBC

## Overview

Anthropic is an AI safety company and frontier model lab, founded by Dario Amodei and others. It is the maker of the Claude family of models (including Claude Opus, Claude Sonnet, and the unreleased "Mythos"). As of April 2026, Anthropic is widely considered the leading lab on model quality benchmarks, having achieved a reported ARR of $35–40B+ (up from $9B) with gross margins expanding from a leaked ~30% to an estimated 72%+ floor.

## Model Trajectory (from source)

- **Claude 4.6 Opus**: Described as roughly L4 software engineer equivalent.
- **Claude 4.7 Opus**: Released April 2026.
- **Mythos**: Internally available February 2026 — equivalent to L6 software engineer. Selectively deployed to top banks for cybersecurity use only. 5–10x token cost of current models, but more token-efficient so often cheaper per task. Full release withheld due to capabilities concerns.

## Competitive Position

Dylan Patel's view: Anthropic leads on model quality but is **compute-constrained**. This is a structural irony — Dario Amodei previously criticized OpenAI for "irresponsible" compute scaling; now Anthropic wishes it had bought far more compute. OpenAI's larger compute base means it will hit comparable model tiers and serve demand Anthropic cannot. Anthropic wins on margins; OpenAI wins on capacity.
