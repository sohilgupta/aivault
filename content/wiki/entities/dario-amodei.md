# Dario Amodei

**Type:** Person  
**Also known as:** CEO of Anthropic; co-founder

## Overview

Dario Amodei is the CEO and co-founder of [[wiki/entities/anthropic]], previously VP of Research at OpenAI. He left OpenAI in 2021 with his sister Daniela Amodei and several other researchers to found Anthropic, motivated by concerns about AI safety practices at OpenAI. He is the primary philosophical and strategic voice of Anthropic — writing long public memos, delivering biweekly company addresses, and giving rare but dense long-form interviews.

## Worldview (from sources)

- **The exponential has 1–2 years left**: After that, AI is a peer to the best human knowledge workers in most cognitive domains.
- **Biological revolution**: The most important near-term application — compressing decades of medical progress into years.
- **LLM path is fundamentally more optimistic** than the competitive-agent path Anthropic started with in 2017–2018. Chain-of-thought interpretability is the key differentiator.
- **Diffusion is real but fast**: AI will diffuse faster than any prior technology, but not instantly. Enterprise adoption lags individual adoption by months.
- **Scaling laws are a bet that keeps paying off**: Every argument he's made about scaling has continued to validate.

## Communication Style

Dario runs biweekly all-hands ("DVQ" — Dario Vision Quest, a name he didn't choose) where he addresses the whole company for ~1 hour with a 3–4 page document covering model progress, products, industry, and geopolitics. He also maintains a Slack channel where he shares unfiltered commentary. His goal: radical internal honesty, no corporate speak.
