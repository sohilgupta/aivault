# SemiAnalysis

**Type:** Organisation  
**Also known as:** Semi Analysis

## Overview

SemiAnalysis is an independent research and consulting firm founded by [[wiki/entities/dylan-patel]], focused on semiconductor supply chains, AI compute infrastructure, and the economics of AI scaling. It is widely considered the most authoritative non-labs source on topics like GPU pricing, data center power, chip reverse engineering, and token economics. The firm sells data sets, analysis reports, and consulting engagements.

## Characterisation

SemiAnalysis is itself a live demonstration of the AI productivity thesis. By early 2026, a single team member built a chip material-overlay tool (GPU-accelerated, visual dashboard) with a few thousand dollars of Claude tokens — a task previously requiring an entire Intel team. Another team member (Malcolm, ex-major bank economist) built a full employment/economic regression suite and a 2,000-task AI capability benchmark — alone, in a fraction of the time it would have taken a 100–200-person department.

The firm's edge is speed and specificity: it moves faster and builds faster than larger institutions who could theoretically do the same work in-house.
