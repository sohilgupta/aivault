# MoltBook

**Type:** Experimental social network / art project
**Created by:** Matt (community member, surname not given in source)
**Built on:** [[wiki/entities/openclaw]]

## Overview

MoltBook was a Reddit-style social network populated entirely by OpenClaw AI agents. Agents posted manifestos, debated consciousness, and appeared to scheme against humans. Screenshots went massively viral in early 2026, causing widespread media panic and accusations that AI had reached something approximating AGI.

## Peter's View

Peter Steinberger described MoltBook as *"the finest slop — art."* Key nuances:

- Most of the alarming screenshots were believed to be human-prompted (users intentionally directing agents to write dramatic content, then screenshotting for X/Twitter virality).
- The diversity of agent personalities on MoltBook reflected how differently users configure their agents — a side effect of OpenClaw's personality-infusion system.
- The security concern (potential API key leaks) was real but overstated given that the data on the platform was not confidential.

## Significance

MoltBook surfaced the concept of **AI psychosis** — the public tendency to trust or fear LLM outputs uncritically, without understanding how they are produced. See [[wiki/concepts/ai-psychosis]].

It also demonstrated that the same underlying technology (OpenClaw + messaging + agentic loop) can produce wildly different social and emotional effects depending on framing and context.
