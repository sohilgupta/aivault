# Krishna Rao

**Type:** Person
**Also known as:** CFO of Anthropic

## Overview

Krishna Rao is CFO of [[wiki/entities/anthropic]]. Joined ~mid-2024 when Anthropic had ~$250M run-rate revenue; has raised ~$75B since, with another $50B+ committed via the late-2026 Amazon/Google deals (combined >$100B compute commitments). Previously led Airbnb's pandemic-era financing and spent earlier years at Blackstone's private equity group. Spends 30–40% of his time on compute strategy.

## Operating Frame (from source)

- **Cone of uncertainty.** Exponential growth makes point estimates useless; plan to be near the top of the cone; raise to cover cone risk, not operating losses.
- **Compute is the canvas.** Three chip platforms used fungibly across model dev, internal acceleration, customer inference. Margin frame is "ROI on compute envelope," not per-token.
- **Returns to frontier intelligence stay very high in enterprise.** Customers switch to newest models immediately; each generation unlocks TAM via long-horizon and tool-use, not raw IQ.
- **Talent density > talent mass.** Lost ~2 researchers to Meta mega-offers; peer labs lost dozens. Culture-interview gating is enforced.
- **Pricing stability + Jevons.** Only major price change was lowering Opus at 4.5; consumption rose far more than the cut.
- **Vision frontier: virtual collaborator.** Context-aware, tool-using, memory-equipped agents executing long-horizon enterprise knowledge work.
