# Satya Nadella

**Type:** Person  
**Also known as:** CEO of Microsoft

## Overview

Satya Nadella has been CEO of Microsoft since 2014. He transformed Microsoft from a declining Windows-centric company into the world's most valuable enterprise cloud provider. The OpenAI partnership (2019–present) is his most consequential bet, giving Microsoft exclusive Azure deployment rights for OpenAI models and co-building Copilot across the Microsoft 365 suite.

## Intellectual Contributions (from sources)

- **Trust as the most important feature**: More important than model capability in many markets. Countries/enterprises need to believe their provider will be there in 10 years.
- **Sovereignty as business requirement**: Microsoft treats sovereignty (data residency, confidential computing, key management) as a first-class engineering + policy problem.
- **Open source as concentration check**: Open source ensures no single model dominates globally. Nations need the option to move.
- **TSMC sovereignty is a scam**: The actual fab capacity in Arizona doesn't come close to replacing TSMC. True sovereignty requires decades of investment — but nations will demand it anyway, and companies must comply.
- **American tech trust advantage**: If US companies can maintain trust (respecting data sovereignty, long-term presence), they may win the global AI race even against Chinese competition on capabilities.
