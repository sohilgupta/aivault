# Airbnb

**Type:** Organisation (public)
**Also known as:** ABNB

## Overview

Airbnb is the home-stays marketplace co-founded by Brian Chesky in 2008. As of 2026, ~$134B annual gross sales, ~40% free cash flow margins, ~5,000 employees. The company is in the middle of an AI-driven redesign: shift the atomic unit from a home to a person; industrialise 50–70 new verticals beyond stays; rebuild the app/agent surface around AI.

## Strategic Direction (2026, per Chesky)

1. **Atomic unit: home → person.** Build the most authenticated identity profile and richest preference library on the internet; real-world social graph + membership program.
2. **Industrialise multi-vertical expansion** via the 1→10→many pilot model. One city first (NYC for Airbnb, SF for Uber, Palo Alto for DoorDash); only scale after PMF.
3. **AI redesign of the app surface.** Innovator's-dilemma constrained because $100B+ flows through the app; exploring separate sandbox/app for radical reinvention. "I don't think there will be apps in the future. I think there'll be agents."
