# Ilya Sutskever

**Type:** Person  
**Also known as:** Co-founder OpenAI; Co-author AlexNet, GPT-3; Founder Safe Superintelligence (SSI)

## Overview

Ilya Sutskever is one of the most influential figures in deep learning history. He co-authored AlexNet (with Hinton and Krizhevsky) — the paper that started the deep learning revolution — and was a principal architect of the scaling strategy at OpenAI that produced GPT-3 and beyond. He was famously involved in the November 2023 board crisis that briefly fired Sam Altman. After reconciliation and a period of uncertainty, he left OpenAI in 2024 to found Safe Superintelligence (SSI), a company focused exclusively on building safe superintelligence with no commercial distractions.

## Intellectual Contributions (from sources)

- **Age of scaling is ending**: Pre-training on internet data has hit diminishing returns. New algorithmic ideas (synthetic data, continual learning, new training paradigms) are needed.
- **Age of research is beginning**: The scarce resource going forward is research *taste* — the ability to identify which ideas are worth pursuing.
- **Continual learning agents**: Human-like incremental learning from experience is a key frontier. Would change the economics of AI deployment.
- **Diversity of AI agents**: All pre-trained LLMs are too similar (trained on same data). RL/post-training is where differentiation starts. Getting genuinely diverse AI researchers requires different training, not just temperature.
- **Self-play found modern form**: Prover-verifier, LLM-as-judge, adversarial debate — not classical game-playing but structurally related.
- **Research taste**: Guided by aesthetic, brain-inspired intuition as top-down prior. Sustains you when experiments fail.
