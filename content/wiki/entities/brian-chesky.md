# Brian Chesky

**Type:** Person
**Also known as:** Co-founder and CEO of Airbnb

## Overview

Brian Chesky is the co-founder and CEO of [[wiki/entities/airbnb]]. RISD-trained industrial designer; the empirical basis for Paul Graham's "Founder Mode" essay. As of 2026 he is articulating "AI Founder Mode" — an even more hands-on, asynchronous, fewer-layered operating system for running a company in the AI era. Coined the 11-Star Experience exercise. Predicts a consumer AI renaissance in the next 12–24 months.

## Operating Frame (from source)

- **Founder Mode → AI Founder Mode.** Pure people managers do not survive; every manager must remain IC-capable (engineers code, lawyers read case law).
- **Make the problem as small as possible.** Project Hawaii pattern: 10–12 person Navy-SEAL teams attacking one problem with crawl-walk-run-fly cadence. One team drove 600+ bps of conversion lift on $134B GMV.
- **11-Star Experience.** Imagine 6–11-star versions of a moment to free imagination; work backwards to a viable 6-star.
- **Atomic unit shift: home → person.** Build the most authenticated identity and richest preference library on the internet; proof-of-personhood matters in the AI age.
- **Hiring is the #1 CEO job.** Pipeline recruiting (constantly meeting talent), not search-based. Start with results, work back to people. Co-hires top 200.
- **Apps → agents.** Software is hyper-fast-fashion; only community, brand, principles, identity endure.
