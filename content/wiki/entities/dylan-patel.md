# Dylan Patel

**Type:** Person  
**Also known as:** Founder of SemiAnalysis

## Overview

Dylan Patel is the founder of [[wiki/entities/semianalysis]], widely regarded as the most technically authoritative independent research firm on AI compute, semiconductor supply chains, and the economics of AI infrastructure. He is a regular source for journalists, investors, and operators trying to understand the physical and financial underpinnings of the AI revolution. His firm operates in the "information business" — selling analysis, consulting, and proprietary data sets on AI infrastructure.

## Characterisation (from source)

Dylan describes himself as being in the "information business" — and frames his firm's survival as requiring constant acceleration: if you don't move faster than your customers can DIY, you will be commoditized. He uses his own firm as a live experiment in AI adoption, and characterizes the leap in capability in early 2026 as genuinely fear-inducing — not because AI is malevolent, but because it threatens to reorder the economy in ways society is not prepared to reason about.

He coined (or popularized within this context) the term **"Claude Code psychosis"** — the moment when a person first deeply uses Claude for a major creative/analytical workflow and their productivity perception is permanently altered.
