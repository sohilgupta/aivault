# Palmer Luckey

**Type:** Person  
**Also known as:** Founder of Oculus VR; Founder and CEO of Anduril Industries

## Overview

Palmer Luckey built the Oculus Rift VR headset prototype at 16, founded Oculus at 18, sold it to Facebook for several billion dollars at ~21, then founded Anduril Industries — a defense technology company focused on AI-powered autonomous weapons systems. He is one of the few Silicon Valley figures who has successfully translated consumer tech founder experience into defense technology, disrupting incumbent defense contractors on cost and capability.

## Intellectual Contributions

- **Non-humanoid combat AI**: The human form is a terrible design for combat. Anduril's autonomous systems are hyper-specialised — designed by function, not anthropomorphic intuition.
- **Multi-sensor verification**: Reliable anomaly detection requires simultaneous radar + camera correlation; single-sensor UFO sightings are insufficient evidence.
- **Defense-tech disruption**: Private-sector discipline can cut DoD costs dramatically because incumbent contractors have no market correction mechanism when they fail.
- **AI for combat training**: VR + AI can simulate specific fighters (at known skill levels) for progressive sparring, potentially better than human sparring partners.
