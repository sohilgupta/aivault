# Andrej Karpathy

**Type:** Person  
**Also known as:** Independent AI educator; former Tesla Autopilot Dir.; former OpenAI research

## Overview

Andrej Karpathy is one of the most respected AI educators alive. He co-authored seminal work with Ilya Sutskever at OpenAI (GPT-era research), led Tesla's Autopilot team, and is now independent — building educational repositories (micrograd, nanoGPT, nanochat) and giving rare, intellectually rich interviews. His defining insight is the pedagogy: knowledge must be "untangled into a ramp where everything depends only on the thing before it." He is the antidote to jargon-first science communication.

## Intellectual Contributions (from sources)

- **LLMs as ghosts**: The most useful reframe — LLMs are a distillation/compression of human writing, not novel intelligences. Summoning a statistical ghost of the corpus.
- **Three coding modes**: Scratch / autocomplete-assisted / vibe-coding agents — each appropriate for different contexts. Agents are good for boilerplate, bad for novel intellectually intense code.
- **Two types of knowledge**: Surface knowledge (can explain) vs deep knowledge (can build). Building is the only way to find your knowledge gaps.
- **Education as knowledge untangling**: Find the minimal, motivated presentation of an idea. Show the pain before the solution.
- **Research taste**: Beauty, simplicity, brain-inspired intuition. "Ugliness has no room." Aesthetic as a top-down prior when experiments fail.
