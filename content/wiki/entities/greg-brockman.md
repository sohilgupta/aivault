# Greg Brockman

**Type:** Person  
**Also known as:** Co-founder and President of OpenAI (on sabbatical as of late 2024)

## Overview

Greg Brockman is a co-founder of OpenAI and long-time President. He was at the centre of Sam Altman's reinstatement crisis in November 2023 — he resigned the same day Sam was fired, signalling to the board the depth of internal loyalty to Sam. Greg has been on sabbatical since late 2024. He returned to the public eye for this Core Memory interview alongside Sam Altman.

## Worldview (from sources)

- The hardest thing isn't any specific technical problem — it's choosing *which* direction to run. "AI is a field of boundless opportunity. No matter what dimension you expand on, there's something new and unprecedented and amazing."
- The personal AGI vision resonates with Greg particularly from a UX perspective: the frustration of constantly re-explaining context to an AI is a real UX problem that kills productivity.
