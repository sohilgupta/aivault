# Leopold Aschenbrenner

**Type:** Person  
**Also known as:** Former OpenAI safety researcher; author of "Situational Awareness"

## Overview

Leopold Aschenbrenner is a former OpenAI safety researcher who wrote "Situational Awareness," an influential document arguing that AGI is coming by 2027 and that the geopolitical/national security implications are being catastrophically underweighted by the AI industry and policymakers. He left OpenAI (reportedly over safety disagreements) and is now focused on policy and the geopolitical dimensions of the AI race.

## Core Thesis

The AI industry is sleepwalking through the most consequential national security transition since WWII. AI is not a product story — it's a Manhattan Project story. The race to intelligence explosion is analogous to the race to the atomic bomb. The US has a lead that must be protected through secrecy, domestic compute cluster location, and espionage defense. A 2-year lead at the intelligence explosion means decades of safety margin; a 3-month lead means feverish, unstable, dangerous competition.
