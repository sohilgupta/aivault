# Elon Musk

**Type:** Person  
**Also known as:** CEO xAI, Tesla, SpaceX; CTO xAI; original OpenAI co-founder (departed 2018)

## Overview

Elon Musk is a co-founder of OpenAI who departed in 2018 over disagreements about control, then founded xAI and Grok as a competitor. He is in ongoing litigation with OpenAI (the "Elon Musk trial" referenced by Sam Altman). His AI thesis is unique: model quality differences are transient (6-month half-life), so the real differentiator is hardware scaling speed. He is building TeraFab (a semiconductor fab), pursuing space-based AI compute, and signing chip deals with Samsung specifically to diversify away from TSMC.

## Intellectual Contributions (from sources)

- **Ideas travel in ~6 months between labs**: Model quality gaps don't persist. Therefore hardware execution speed is the real variable.
- **Space compute in 36 months**: Solar is abundant, free, no fuel costs. Radiation-tolerant chips are solvable. Radiator mass is the dominant engineering challenge. Net energy cost approaches zero.
- **TeraFab**: Building to ~1M wafers/month by 2030 with logic + memory + packaging. Starting small, making mistakes at scale before scaling.
- **Energy is the 1-year bottleneck**: By end of 2025 chip output exceeds ability to turn chips on. xAI's hardware competency = can turn on chips others can't.
- **High pain threshold as competitive advantage**: Willingness to lean into acute pain (build the fab, do the hard thing) to solve bottlenecks.
