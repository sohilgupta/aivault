# Peter Steinberger

**Type:** Person
**Also known as:** The Clawfather, @steipete (Twitter)

## Overview

Peter Steinberger is an Austrian software developer and entrepreneur, creator of [[wiki/entities/openclaw]], and previously the founder of [[wiki/entities/pspdfkit]]. He is widely recognised as a leading figure in the practical, open-source side of the AI agent revolution. His philosophy centres on building for joy, playing to learn, and treating agents as capable engineering colleagues rather than magic wands.

## Career Arc

1. **PSPDFKit** — Founded and ran for 13 years. PDF SDK used on a billion devices. Burned out, primarily from people management stress rather than coding itself. Sold; then detached from programming for ~3 years.
2. **Sabbatical** — One-way ticket to Madrid. Reconnected with life. Fell back in love with building.
3. **Experimentation phase** — 2025: Year of learning agents. Built many small tools, CLI utilities, explored WhatsApp+LLM integrations.
4. **OpenClaw** — November 2025: one-hour prototype (WhatsApp relay → Claude Code CLI). 2026: exploded virally.

## Philosophy

- "Agentic engineering" (disciplined, architectural) vs. "vibe coding" (post-3AM regret mode).
- Design code for agents to navigate, not for your own preferences.
- Treat agents like capable team members: give context, trust their judgment, then review the output.
- Play is the best way to learn. Ship to main. Refactor after building.
- Don't take yourself too seriously. Fun is a competitive advantage.
- Values money as affirmation, not as goal. Has a foundation for helping disadvantaged people.
