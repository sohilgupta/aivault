# Sundar Pichai

**Type:** Person  
**Also known as:** CEO of Google and Alphabet

## Overview

Sundar Pichai is the CEO of Google and its parent company Alphabet. He joined Google in 2004 and is known for overseeing Chrome, Android, and then rising to CEO in 2015. His leadership style is calm, long-term oriented, and institutionally aware — he frequently cites Google's history of building things slowly (Waymo, TPUs, Transformer research) that eventually become dominant.

## Worldview (from sources)

- **AI is not zero-sum**: "The value of what people are going to be able to do is also on some crazy curve." YouTube survived TikTok. Google can lead AI without crushing everyone else.
- **Search evolves, not dies**: Search becomes an agent manager — asynchronous, multi-threaded, task-completing. The word "search" may not survive, but the capability expands.
- **Speed is a product philosophy, not just engineering**: Latency budgets as granular as 3 milliseconds. This is a cultural artefact of what makes Google products feel different.
- **Consumer internet always has surprises**: The Transformer was built inside Google Brain but wasn't the main priority. YouTube happened. Instagram happened. You can't predict which garage startup becomes dominant — but you also can't ship a better iPhone from a garage.
