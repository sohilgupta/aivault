# AGI Timeline

## Definition

The "AGI timeline" concept tracks the specific predictions made by key figures in this wiki about when AI will reach peer-level human cognitive capability (AGI) and superintelligence. This is the most consequential empirical question any of these sources address. The predictions cluster tightly around 2026-2028 but carry very different implications depending on the speaker's frame.

## What "AGI" Means (Contested)

The figures in this wiki don't use a single definition:
- **Leopold**: Peer-level researcher across all domains → triggers intelligence explosion
- **Dario**: Peer-level cognitive worker (not researcher) — a software engineer peer, not a scientist
- **Sam**: Personal AGI — an AI that knows everything about you and acts on your behalf
- **Ilya**: Doesn't use "AGI" — frames it as the end of the scaling paradigm, not a threshold event

## The Intelligence Explosion (Leopold's frame)

Once peer-level AI researchers exist, they can directly accelerate their own development. "A billion superintelligent researchers" working simultaneously on alignment, capabilities, and engineering. Progress that would take humans years takes them hours. The resulting capability jump is potentially "five orders of magnitude" above human level. This is the scenario that makes the US-China race a national security emergency — being one year behind could mean being outpaced permanently.

## Why This Matters

The timeline predictions are not academic. They determine:
- **How urgently** to invest in safety vs. capabilities
- **Whether** the US-China race has years or months of wiggle room
- **What kind** of preparation is appropriate (product roadmaps vs. national mobilisation)
- **How soon** the biological revolution actually arrives

## Open Questions

- Will the transition to peer-level AI be a sharp step-function (Leopold's explosion framing) or a smooth gradient (Dario's end-of-exponential framing)?
- Does the intelligence explosion require **recursive self-improvement in practice** or is it simply the aggregate effect of many AI researchers working in parallel?
- What is the right safety preparation strategy if you genuinely believe 2027?
