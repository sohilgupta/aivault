# Phantom GDP

## Definition

Phantom GDP is the economic value created by AI-driven productivity gains that does not appear in standard GDP statistics — because the output is delivered at dramatically lower cost (often approaching zero marginal cost), the price deflation is not captured as a real gain, and the beneficiaries are often not the firms generating the output.

The term was coined by Malcolm (economist at SemiAnalysis, formerly of a major bank's economist department) while building an AI-assisted economic analysis suite for [[wiki/entities/semianalysis]].

## How It Appears in This Wiki

This concept is new to this wiki, introduced by [[wiki/sources/dylan-patel-token-supply-demand]]. It is a macro-economic measurement problem that sits at the intersection of AI adoption and economic statistics.

## The Mechanism

GDP measures output at market prices. When AI enables:
- One person to do the work of a 200-person economist team
- Software to compress months of chip reverse-engineering into a few thousand dollars
- A single energy analyst to build a grid model that rivals a 100-person company's decade of work

...the *output* goes up, but the *price* falls to near-zero or is not captured in market transactions at all. If information that used to sell for $X is now produced for $X/100 and sold at $X/10, the volume of economic activity that GDP tracks goes down even as real-world value increases.

> "Output can go up. It's called phantom GDP. Output can go up, but because cost falls so much, actually GDP theoretically shrinks." — Malcolm (paraphrased via Dylan Patel)

## Why It Matters

- **Policymakers** using GDP to assess AI's economic impact will systematically undercount it.
- **Central banks** calibrating for inflation or deflation may miss a deflationary wave already baked in.
- **Investors** evaluating AI companies on revenue alone miss the shadow value being distributed throughout the economy.
- **Social policy** debates on AI and employment will be poorly informed if the value is invisible.

## Connection to Token Economics

Phantom GDP is the flip side of [[wiki/concepts/tokenomics]]: tokens generate economic value. Much of that value is non-market (internal productivity, better decisions, faster research) or deeply discounted relative to the old cost of producing equivalent output. The people capturing phantom GDP are those early in adopting token arbitrage — which is why Dylan frames it as connected to the [[wiki/concepts/ai-permanent-underclass]] thesis.
