## Key Claims

- **SOPs as the new resume**: Vaibhav argues the future of hiring won't be CVs — it will be your SOP files (markdown documents of your systematised processes). Your experience = your markdown files. "Experience is the markdown files."
- **Skill MD files**: Knowledge workers should document their processes as `.md` files — these become the AI-readable context for automating their own work.
- **The unfair advantage of starting now**: Everyone will be doing AI-assisted work in 2 years. Starting now builds compounding expertise. First-mover advantage in personal AI adoption is real.
- **Claude as the recommended tool**: Vaibhav specifically recommends Claude Code / Claude API for practical AI work.
- **Agentic workflows for business**: Businesses should be building SOPs that AI can execute, not just using AI ad-hoc. The distinction between "AI as a tool I use" vs "AI running my processes" is key.
- **The next podcast will be about jobs**: Vaibhav frames a follow-up episode on (a) which jobs will be automated, (b) how individuals can compete, (c) building your AI "second brain."
