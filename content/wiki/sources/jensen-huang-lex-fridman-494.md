## Summary

A longer, more personal Jensen Huang interview than the Dwarkesh one. The Lex conversation goes into mortality, succession planning, Jensen's hope for humanity, and the biological revolution. The key new contribution is Jensen's explicit alignment with Dario Amodei's biological revolution thesis — "understanding the biological machine is right around the corner, maybe 5 years" — and his vision of uploading consciousness into a humanoid and sending it to the stars.

---

## Key Claims

- **Understanding the biological machine in 5 years**: "It's not 10 years. It's 5 years probably." Jensen explicitly aligns with Dario's biological revolution thesis, adding theoretical physics as a second domain that will be unlocked.
- **Succession planning misconception**: Jensen says he doesn't believe in succession planning. The correct response to mortality is to pass on knowledge continuously and in real time. Every meeting is a reasoning meeting. Nothing he learns sits on his desk for more than a fraction of a second before he's pointing others to it.
- **Groq acquisition for premium-latency tokens**: (Confirms Dwarkesh interview claim.) Different customers have different willingness to pay for speed. The inference market is disaggregating.
- **Passion for humanity over technology**: Jensen's stated motivation is not AI or chips — it's that AI makes human potential more realizable. "The end of disease is a reasonable thing to expect." Cancer, pollution, physics of travel.
- **The consciousness upload vision**: Jensen's personal death plan: upload consciousness via internet footprint (inbox, everything said/written) into an AI, send that AI in a humanoid on a spaceship. "Just send that at the speed of light, catch up with my robot."
- **Nvidia as a once-in-humanity experience**: "Not once in a lifetime — once in humanity."
