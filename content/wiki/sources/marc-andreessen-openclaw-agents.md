## Summary

Marc Andreessen discusses the future of software abstractions, why HTTP 402 (payment required) was the missing internet primitive, and why AI + crypto stablecoins are about to unify. He specifically discusses OpenClaw and "Pi" (Peter Steinberger), observing that the bleeding-edge OpenClaw users have already given their agents bank accounts and credit cards. This is the future distribution level for personal agents.

---

## Key Claims

- **AI agents need money**: The grand unification is AI + crypto/stablecoins. HTTP 402 (payment required) was the missing primitive from the original internet. Now we have internet-native money and AI agents that need to transact. The two are converging.
- **OpenClaw users giving agents bank accounts**: Marc's most aggressive AI-using friends have already given their OpenClaw instances bank accounts and credit cards. This is the obvious next step — an agent that can't spend money on your behalf is severely limited. Currently ~5,000 people doing this, but it'll grow.
- **Model lock-in is more subtle than people think**: If you build software with Claude vs. OpenAI and then want to switch models, the second model can reverse-engineer the first's output. Like reverse engineering x86 binaries today — humans can do it, it's just cost-prohibitive; models can do it efficiently. So model lock-in is less durable than it appears.
- **Death of the browser as abstraction**: AI agents don't need a browser as an interface. They interact directly at the API/protocol level. The browser was built to compensate for human limitations (reading slowly, navigating menus). Remove those limitations and the abstraction disappears.
- **Future will be "tell the thing what I need"**: No programming languages matter if AI generates optimal code. You just describe the outcome. The optimal internal representation will be whatever the model decides — humans won't need to understand it.
- **Founder-led companies with AI superpowers**: Marc's best current frame for the future of organisations. The innovation problem of large companies solved by AI doing the management layer.
