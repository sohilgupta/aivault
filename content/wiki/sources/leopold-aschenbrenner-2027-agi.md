## Summary

Leopold Aschenbrenner — former OpenAI safety researcher, author of the influential "Situational Awareness" document — gives the most geopolitically focused AI interview in the wiki. His core argument: AI is not a product story, it's a **national security** story. We are approaching an "intelligence explosion" that will compress decades of scientific progress into years. The US-China race around this inflection point is the most important event in post-WWII history. The primary risk is not misalignment — it's that China gets there first, or that compute clusters get built in authoritarian countries that can seize or exfiltrate them.

This is the most consequential strategic frame in the wiki. Every other source (Dario, Elon, Satya, Jensen) touches national security tangentially; Leopold makes it the central thesis.

---

## Key Claims

- **2027 AGI**: Within ~2 years, we will have AI systems that are peer-level researchers across all domains. This is not "smarter autocomplete" — it's a phase transition in the nature of intelligence itself.
- **Intelligence explosion**: Once peer-level AI researchers exist, they will dramatically accelerate their own research. A year might be the difference between human-level AI and a system that is "five orders of magnitude" smarter. "A billion superintelligent scientists" all working on alignment, capabilities, and engineering simultaneously.
- **The Manhattan Project analogy**: The secrecy question around AI breakthroughs (what is the next paradigm after pre-training?) is directly analogous to the graphite moderator secrecy decision. Szilard's insistence on secrecy likely delayed the German bomb by years. The US has a similar decision to make now.
- **Compute clusters must stay in the US and allied democracies**: Building AGI clusters in Middle Eastern autocracies is catastrophic risk. They can: (a) exfiltrate the weights (steal the AGI), (b) seize the compute, (c) gain implicit political leverage. "Would you do the Manhattan Project in the UAE?"
- **The dangerous scenario is a close race**: If the US has a 3-month lead and China is right behind, both sides have no margin for safety — maximum speed, maximum risk, unstable deterrence with rapidly changing WMDs. The safe scenario is a US lead of 2+ years, analogous to US vs Germany in WWII.
- **100 GW is doable in the US if we're willing to use natural gas**: West Texas, southwest Pennsylvania (Marcellus Shale). The 10 GW cluster is "super easy." 100 GW is doable. The challenge is not physical possibility — it's political will and build speed.
- **Espionage is the primary near-term threat**: China has stolen more American IP than any other country. The weights of frontier models are the most valuable IP ever created. Current lab security is inadequate for what's coming.
- **The quiet period is ending**: COVID showed that even the president and everyone eventually pivots to focus on a single dominating event. Soon it will be AGI. "This is the quiet period." Make plans accordingly.

## Contradictions / Tensions

- Leopold is more alarmed than any other source in the wiki about timeline (2027). Dario says "1-2 years" to peer-level cognitive worker, which is consistent. Ilya says age of research is beginning, implying more than 1-2 years. The most alarmed and the most cautious sources actually agree on the fact of the transition; they differ on what it means.
- Leopold's national security framing could conflict with OpenAI's and Anthropic's desire to develop commercially and globally. If this is a wartime mobilisation, who controls the labs?
