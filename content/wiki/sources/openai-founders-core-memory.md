## Summary

Sam Altman and Greg Brockman appear together on the Core Memory podcast hosted by Ashley Vance to cover OpenAI's history, current state, and plans. The conversation is wide-ranging: from the origin story of ChatGPT (not their most impressive technical feat, but the first thing people could *feel*), to the Elon Musk trial (which Sam frames as an opportunity to finally tell the real story), to compute strategy, to the vision of a personal AI that knows everything about you.

The episode is less analytically dense than Dario's Dwarkesh interview, but rich in narrative and framing. Sam and Greg are clearly coordinating on a public narrative pivot: from defensive silence about the Elon split to active, proud articulation of what OpenAI actually stands for.

---

## Key Claims

- **ChatGPT was not OpenAI's most impressive technical feat** — but it was the first thing people could *feel*, and that changed everything. Prior blog posts, robot hands doing Rubik's cubes, game-playing AI — no one cared until they could use it themselves.
- **Coding is the second major "aha" moment** for mass adoption (after ChatGPT). These are the two moments the world has collectively updated on AI.
- **The personal AGI that knows you** is the next frontier: a system that knows your context, your life, your relationships, your computer, your browser — so you never have to re-explain yourself. Sam is "tremendously excited" and admits even they don't have good intuition for what this will feel like.
- **The Elon trial**: Sam wants to have it. The real breaking point was Elon demanding absolute control over OpenAI — "that was the thing that caused us to say no." OpenAI has stayed silent for years; the trial forces them to tell the real story.
- **AGI path shift**: In 2017–2018, the plan was competitive multi-agent simulation. That produces a powerful, alien intelligence with no connection to human values. The LLM path is fundamentally different and more optimistic — rooted in language, rooted in human values.
- **Too much opportunity**: Greg frames OpenAI's biggest technical challenge as having *too many* directions to explore. The critical skill is focus — where to concentrate for maximum impact.
- **Compute is the fundamental constraint**: Not stated as explicitly as Dylan Patel, but implicit throughout — both in Elon's original demands (he wanted to bring compute) and in the current strategy.
- **Image generation has a step-change coming**: Sam teases an upcoming image model that made him realise "wow, this can go so much further" — even though he thought image generation was "solved."
