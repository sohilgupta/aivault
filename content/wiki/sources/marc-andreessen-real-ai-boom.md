## Summary

Marc Andreessen makes the most contrarian and structurally sophisticated case for AI diffusion *resistance* in the wiki. His core claim: both AI utopians and AI doomers are far too optimistic about the *speed* of change, because they assume that technology making something possible immediately leads to 8 billion people changing behaviour. It does not. The existing economy is "wired in" with unions, professional licensing cartels, government monopolies, and civil service protections that will delay or outright block AI adoption in the largest sectors of the economy — healthcare, education, legal, government.

This is the most important counter-balance to the diffusion optimism in sources like Dario, Sundar, and Satya.

---

## Key Claims

- **Both utopians and doomers are too optimistic about speed**: They believe technology making something possible = people will change. "8 billion people all of a sudden changing how they behave — just no."
- **35% of the economy requires professional certification**: Healthcare, legal, hairdressing — these are cartels. AI cannot displace them politically even if it can technically.
- **K-12 education will not change**: It's a literal government monopoly with teachers 100% opposed. "There's no chance." The only path is building an entirely new school system (like Alpha School).
- **Dock workers won**: They have 25,000 active workers + 25,000 drawing full paychecks from prior agreements (not working). They struck and won — operators committed to not adding more automation. Even a 25,000-person union has enormous political power.
- **Government agencies struck COVID agreements for office 1 day/month**: These are locked in. Buildings empty 58/60 days are still being paid for. You can't AI your way out of civil service protections.
- **There will be massive slippage**: The AI transformation will take far longer in the real economy than the tech optimists believe. We'll be lucky if adoption happens quickly. If it doesn't, we get stagnation.
- **Founder-led + AI might be the winning formula**: The new Henry Ford: founder with vision + AI doing all the managerial work (paperwork, forms, reports, reading). "Maybe the secret formula has been hidden from us because we never knew it was possible."
- **The Elon method is clearly working**: The only method generating results better than all others. Hard to replicate — maybe needs to be above "30 milliElons."
- **Starlink as the studied non-precedent**: Two prior satellite internet attempts (Teledesic by Gates+McCaw, Iridium by Motorola) both failed catastrophically. Elon did it as a SpaceX side project. Starlink hit 10M subscribers.
